About RaktaSeva

A college demonstration project inspiring voluntary blood donation through meaningful rewards and community recognition.

Our Mission

RaktaSeva was built with a single purpose: to bridge the gap between voluntary blood donors and recognition. By partnering with temples to offer free VIP entry passes, we create a meaningful reward system that encourages more people to donate blood regularly.

Blood donation is one of the most selfless acts a person can perform. A single donation can save up to three lives. RaktaSeva ensures that every donor's contribution is acknowledged, verified, and rewarded.

⚠️ This is a college demonstration concept. Real-world deployment would require official hospital and temple partnerships.
